Welcome to Next Generation Institute

Next Generation Institute Of Computer & Technology gives great importance to providing high-quality vocational skill training and non-formal academic curriculum-based training programs to students.

By joining a career course with Next Generation Institute Of Computer & Technology, you get:

  • A wide variety of career, professional, short-term & certification courses designed for the learning & career needs of students, working professionals & others.
  • Workshops, Events & other activities to encourage student-industry interaction, prepare students for their job interviews & make them industry-ready.
