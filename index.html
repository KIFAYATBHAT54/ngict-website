ABOUT OUR INSTITUTE

Learn. Develop. Succeed.

Next Generation Institute Of Computer & Technology gives great importance to providing high-quality vocational skill training and non-formal academic curriculum-based training programs to students.

By joining a career course with NGICT, students get access to career, professional, short-term and certification programs designed around modern learning and career needs.

  • A wide variety of career, professional, short-term and certification courses designed for students and working professionals.
  • Workshops and activities encouraging student-industry interaction.
  • Interview preparation and industry-oriented learning.
